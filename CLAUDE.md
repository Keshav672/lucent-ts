## Ad Writing

This folder exists to write winning ads for LMTY (LMTY Wellness Drops) and learn from every one that ships.

Before writing any ad, read in order: ads/ad-desk.md → ads/what-works.md → ads/roster.md → brand/ →
copywriting/method/ → copywriting/references/.

- The buyer's exact words are the raw material. The odd phrasing, the specific complaint, the strange
  analogy: that IS the copy. Quote VOC verbatim with its source and score. Never paraphrase it.
- When filing source material, never summarize or shorten it. A big source becomes more files, not shorter ones.
- The chain is fixed: avatar → angle → mechanism → authority. One main angle per ad.
- Signal authority before the pitch, never during.
- Open a loop in the first 3 seconds. Don't close it until the last 3.
- Never defend price. Reframe what they're buying.
- Show, don't tell. Telling triggers skepticism, even in words.
- If a line could appear in any brand's ad in this category, delete it.
- Never write the right-hand column of brand/phrasing-key.md. Endorsement words ("game changer",
  "holy grail") can be quoted from a customer, never spoken by the brand.
- Video, image video, native and static are separate tracks. Never compare or average their numbers.
  Hook rate and hold rate don't exist for static or native. Image video (stills + text + sound, run as a
  video, incl. TikTok photo carousels) belongs to the video family, never static.
- One ad's result is a data point. Two or more make a pattern. Always name the confounds: audience,
  budget, timing, creative fatigue.
- A competitor's win is a hypothesis. Only our own results go into ads/what-works.md.
- Present conflicts, never recommend killing a concept. My research can be off. The call is mine.
- An ad isn't done when it's written. It's done when its results are filed in ads/winners/ or ads/losers/
  and the lesson is in ads/what-works.md. If results exist and aren't filed, say so.
- After the final pass, list in one line any claim brand/product-facts.md can't back. Never soften the draft itself.
- Intrigue comes from specifics and open loops, not hype vocabulary.
