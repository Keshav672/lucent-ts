# LMTY Wellness Drops: ultra-realistic image prompt for Gemini

## How to use
1. Open Gemini (gemini.google.com) and start a new chat. Use the image model ("Nano Banana" / Gemini image generation).
2. Paste **PART 1** once. Wait for image 1 and approve it (regenerate until the label is perfect).
3. Then paste each line from **PART 2** one at a time, in the same chat, so Gemini keeps the exact same bottle.
4. Download each image as soon as it's made and rename it to the file name shown.
5. Send all of them back here (loose files or zipped). Names can be wrong; they'll be matched by content.

The Gemini app cannot hand you one zip file, so download as you go. If you use Google AI Studio instead, the same prompts work and you can download all images at the end.

**Best realism of all:** if you have a real sample bottle, photograph it on a kitchen counter by a window with your phone and send that photo. Then start Part 1 with "Use the attached photo of my real bottle" and Gemini will build every scene around the real product.

## PART 1 (paste once)

```
You are a commercial product photographer. I need a set of photos for an online store that must be indistinguishable from real photographs. Nobody should be able to tell they are AI-generated.

THE PRODUCT (identical in every image):
A 60 ml amber glass Boston-round dropper bottle with a black rubber-bulb dropper cap and a ribbed black collar. Matte uncoated cream paper label wrapped around the middle, with slight real-world imperfections (tiny edge lift, faint paper texture). Label text, printed crisply in black unless noted, spelled exactly:
- "LMTY" (large bold sans-serif)
- a thin gold line
- "WELLNESS DROPS" (small bold capitals)
- "Lemon flavor" (in muted amber)
- a small round gold seal: "ZERO SUGAR"
- bottom line: "2 FL OZ · 60 mL · 30 SERVINGS"
No other words or logos anywhere on the bottle.

REALISM RULES (follow in every image):
- Shot on a full-frame camera (Sony A7 IV) with a 50mm or 90mm macro lens, f/2.8–f/5.6, ISO 200. Real optical depth of field and natural lens falloff.
- Natural daylight from a window, or one large softbox. Neutral, true-to-life white balance. NOT orange, NOT golden-hour glow, NOT a warm filter over everything.
- Real materials: visible glass reflections of the room/window, refraction through the amber glass, a little dust, fingerprints or a faint drip on the bottle, real paper grain, real wood or stone grain.
- Real imperfection: slightly messy styling, a crumb, a slightly crooked napkin, uneven lemon cuts, natural shadows that are not perfectly soft.
- Subtle film grain, no oversmoothing, no glowing edges, no plastic skin, no HDR look, no bokeh balls, no steam that looks painted.
- Hands (when shown): natural, relaxed, correct anatomy with five fingers, visible skin texture, short unpolished or neutral nails, small wrinkles at the knuckles.
- Colors: mostly neutral whites, light wood, soft greys, with the lemon yellow and amber bottle as the only strong colors.
- No text overlays, captions, watermarks or borders.

Make IMAGE 1 now:
lmty-drops-main.jpg — 1:1 square. The bottle standing upright, label facing the camera, on a light oak kitchen counter next to a window. A real lemon cut in half (one half slightly dried at the edge) and a small sprig of mint beside it. A plain white ceramic mug in the soft background with a hint of real steam. Daylight from the left. Sharp focus on the label so every word is readable.

After you show it, check it yourself: is every word on the label spelled exactly as listed? Does it look like a real photo? If not, fix it and show me again. Then wait for my next message.
```

## Text graphics (don't ask Gemini for these)
The benefits, directions, comparison chart and guarantee images have text on them. Gemini often misspells words in graphics, so those four will be rebuilt automatically with crisp text around your new bottle photo (image 1 or image 6).

## Self-check before sending
- Label on every bottle reads exactly: LMTY / WELLNESS DROPS / Lemon flavor / ZERO SUGAR / 2 FL OZ · 60 mL · 30 SERVINGS
- No orange tint over the whole image; whites look white
- Hands have five fingers and real skin
- No extra words, logos or watermarks
