# Format criteria

Layer these on whichever mode is running.

## Video
Map in seconds. The first 3 seconds are a separate creative product. Map: 0–3s words and visual (what stops the scroll) · beat map by function · time-to-product · time-to-demo · demo construction (one take or cut, hands in frame, jump cut at the reveal) · pacing · register · whether captions duplicate the audio or add a second argument · creator · length strategy (same script at several lengths is a test).
**Metrics:** hook rate (3s), hold rate, thumb-stop, CPM, ROAS, days running.

## Image video
Stills + text + sound run as a video (TikTok carousels too). Video family, never static, never averaged with talking-head video. Map slide by slide: slide 1 = the hook · one job per slide · exact text per slide · the bridge between slides (ellipsis, a split sentence, a cliffhanger) · seconds per slide · what the sound adds · is the proof visible in one frame · phone-real or styled.

## Native
Not an ad, a READ. It must survive the "this is an ad" realisation. Map: entry promise and how fast the page pays it off · reader contract · scroll checkpoints (where would they quit, what pulls them past) · story spine (personal story, listicle, exposé, Q&A, "I tried it") · where the product enters (% down) · mechanism teaching · proof stack order · objection sequence · CTA repetition · offer construction. Natives usually carry the offer. No hook rate.
**Metrics:** time on page, scroll depth, click-to-CTA, prelander → product page conversion.

## Static
No time dimension; map the EYE PATH (1st, 2nd, 3rd, exit) · the single thumb-stop element · overlay text and how many words get read · image–copy relationship (contradiction usually beats redundancy) · proof carried visually · realism · legibility at ~30% size, muted, mid-scroll. Its job is the click. No hook or hold rate.
**Metrics:** CTR, CPM, CPC, outbound click rate, frequency before fatigue.
