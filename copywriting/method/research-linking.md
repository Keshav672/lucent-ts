# Research Linking: the brainstorm

Runs after the questions are answered, before any copywriting reference is opened.
**NO WRITING:** no hooks, no lines, no "something like…". No length limit: pull every piece of VOC that relates. This is a conversation; I improve it before we move on.

Sweep everything, not just the obvious folder. Our own ads first (ad-desk, what-works, roster: has this been tried, and what happened?), then voice-of-customer, reviews, phrasing-key, objections, product-facts, competitors, references, winners and losers.

## Output in two halves

### Half one: the research that relates
1. VOC verbatim with scores, grouped by the point it supports
2. Their own words for this exact thing (labels, metaphors, phrasing)
3. Product facts that support it
4. Product facts that CONSTRAIN it (what's false, absent, or owned by a rival)
5. What our own buyers say (reviews, ad comments). First-party outranks community research.
6. Competitor evidence: who ran this, for how long, and where the territory is empty
7. Tensions and risks: where the concept collides with something documented

### Half two: the ideas
8. Angles that fall out of the research, each tied to its evidence
9. Mechanism routes
10. Proof routes, and whether a skeptic would believe each
11. Directions worth considering, including ones I didn't ask for
12. What we'd need that we don't have. Ask HERE, never later as a note on a draft.

## Rules
- Verbatim always. Say when evidence is thin ("one comment, unscored" vs "1,300 upvotes").
- Our own performance outranks everything.
- Surface contradictions, don't resolve them. Present conflicts; never recommend killing the concept.
- If the concept gets redirected, re-run this whole step.
