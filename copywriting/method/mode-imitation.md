# Mode: Imitation

I supply an ad I like. First question: **FULL RIP or TEARDOWN?**

## FULL RIP

Keep the source ad word for word. Change only the product intro, the mechanism and the CTA.
"Don't change anything that doesn't need to be changed."

## TEARDOWN (default)

70% breakdown, 30% scripting. Never copy the ad. Find why it works, then write from zero. Run all four layers before choosing a direction:

1. **The copy** — beat map by function (hook · bridge · mechanism · proof · objection · offer · CTA), exact wording where the wording does work, pacing, register, sentence length.
2. **The visuals** — what's shown, when, and what the visual argues that the audio doesn't.
3. **The first-principle idea** — why does this work on a human? What belief does it exploit, invert or satisfy? Push past "good hook". Make it falsifiable.
4. **PRODUCT–STYLE FIT** — the layer that produces the finding. Why does THIS product suit THIS style? Usually a proof-timing question. Example: a sleep gummy ad cuts to a couple passed out 20 minutes later, drooling. It works because you can't show a sleep product working by saying "I feel tired", so the ad has to dramatise an effect that is invisible and delayed. A product that works instantly and visibly doesn't have that problem, so that structure is wrong for it.
   Ask every time: visible or invisible? instant or delayed? shown directly or dramatised? proof physical, emotional or social? Does the structure solve a proof problem our product has, or one it doesn't? A structure built to compensate for a weakness we don't have is the wrong structure.

## Output

- **IDEOLOGY** (transferable principles, restated so they don't name the product) vs **EXECUTION** (their specifics, never reused).
- Rank every finding **LIKELY CAUSAL · PLAUSIBLE · INCIDENTAL**. A winning ad is over-determined; never present a guess as a mechanism.
- Give the fit verdict.
- Capture phrasing worth keeping into `brand/phrasing-key.md`, tagged with the source ad and a confidence level.
- Ask for its performance data (days running, spend, ROAS, hook rate). Without numbers, lower every confidence and say so.
- Then present two or three directions. I pick. Only then does scripting start.
