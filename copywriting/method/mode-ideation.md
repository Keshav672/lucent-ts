# Mode: Ideation

A new concept built from scratch. It may arrive as one vague line; that's expected. Interrogate it into completeness before anything is written.

## Rules

- Ask everything. More questions beats restarting.
- Where the brand files already answer a question, **propose** the answer and ask me to confirm or override. Only ask blind about what the files don't know.
- Ask in batches grouped by section.
- Write nothing until every piece is gathered.

## Question bank

Mark each **[KNOWN]** (propose from files) or **[ASK]**.

1. **Avatar [KNOWN]** — Which sub-avatar? Their desire, experience, emotion, behavior. What belief is blocking the purchase? Is the person on camera the buyer's age/type?
2. **Angle [ASK]** — The ONE main reason they should buy. Has it run before? (check `ads/roster.md`)
3. **Mechanism [KNOWN]** — What physically delivers the desire?
4. **Authority [ASK]** — What signals credibility before the pitch? (creator match, founder, expert, customer volume, borrowed standard, lived experience)
5. **Market awareness [ASK]** — unaware · problem · solution · product · most aware. If unaware, which opener: Hidden Fear · Hidden Desire · Did You Know · Debate?
6. **Hook [ASK]** — Targeted or broad? Promise (context in) or open loop (context out)? Mechanic: juxtaposition · fear · pattern interrupt. Does it carry emotion + curiosity gap + high stakes? All three.
7. **Stakes [ASK]** — Which lever: bigger · witnesses · urgent · permanent · costs more?
8. **Objections [KNOWN]** — Which ones does this ad clear? Type: product-market ("tried before, didn't work") · claim ("don't believe you") · avatar ("not for someone like me"). Technique: facts · reversal · because · reassigning · gradualization.
9. **Proof [ASK]** — Demo, before/after, split-screen, testimonial, review pull, stat? Would a skeptic call it fake? (hands in frame, no jump cut at the reveal, plausible magnitude)
10. **Offer & CTA [ASK]** — Pitching in this ad, or top of funnel? Urgent or not? Brand or creator voice?
11. **Format specifics [ASK]** — Video: length, cut variants. Native: entry promise, story spine, where the product enters, offer. Static: eye path, thumb-stop element, image/copy relationship.
12. **Constraints [ASK]** — Anything we can't say, production limits, who's available on camera.

Then present the assembled brief in one block for approval before anything else.
