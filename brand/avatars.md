# Avatars

## A1 — The café-cup regular (primary)
- **Who:** women ~18–34, US, colder states; buys Starbucks or similar 2–4× a week; knows the honey-citrus "medicine ball".
- **Desire:** the warm honey-lemon café cup whenever she wants it, without the $5 and the drive.
- **Experience:** lines at the drive-thru, $4–6 a cup, sweet drinks, buys it most when she's run down or it's cold.
- **Emotion:** wants comfort and a little self-care; mild guilt about café spending.
- **Behavior:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, Pinterest; Target/Amazon shopper; saves recipes.
- **Blocking belief:** "A $20 bottle of drops can't taste like the café cup" / "drops are for water, like MiO."

## A2 — The on-the-go worker
- **Who:** nurses, teachers, office workers, students, travelers.
- **Desire:** something warm and better than plain water during a long shift, with no kitchen.
- **Experience:** break-room kettles, sad tea bags, cold coffee, vending machines.
- **Emotion:** tired, wants a small reward that takes no effort.
- **Behavior:** carries a big water bottle; buys things that fit in a bag or locker.
- **Blocking belief:** "I don't have time for a tea ritual."

## A3 — The gift buyer (seasonal)
- **Desire:** a small "stay cozy / feel better" care-package item.
- **Blocking belief:** "Will they actually use it?"
