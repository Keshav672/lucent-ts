## Empty territory
- A **warm, cozy-cup ritual in a portable dropper**. Water drops own cold/flavored water; tea brands own bags and cartons; nobody owns "the café cozy cup in your bag".
- "Desk / shift / carry-on" occasions for warm lemon tea.
