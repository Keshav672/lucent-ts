# Native ad grading criteria (written 2026-11-14, BEFORE viewing the Facebook Ad Library)

Scope: Facebook/Instagram native ads = long-form primary text that reads like a post, plus headline, description and CTA button.
Score each ad out of 100. Two graders' rule: score the ad as a scrolling stranger would meet it, muted, on a phone.

| # | Criterion | Points | Full marks when… | Zero when… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook (first ~125 characters, before "…See more")** | 20 | Starts in the middle of something specific; opens a curiosity gap; carries emotion and real stakes; a stranger wants the next line. | Brand name, generic claim, question nobody asked, or emoji wall. |
| 2 | **Readability** | 15 | Grade 6 or lower (Flesch-Kincaid); median sentence ≤ 12 words; one idea per line; white space; no jargon. | Dense paragraphs, grade 10+. |
| 3 | **Specificity / visual words** | 15 | Concrete numbers, times, places, objects you can picture ("14 minutes in the drive-thru"). | Abstract words ("elevate", "wellness journey", "game-changing"). |
| 4 | **Voice & register** | 10 | One consistent human speaker; sounds said out loud; uses buyer words, not brand words. | Corporate tone, mixed registers, hype. |
| 5 | **Mechanism** | 10 | Says in plain words WHY it works / why it's different. | No reason given, or vague "formula". |
| 6 | **Proof & believability** | 10 | Falsifiable claims, numbers the reader can check, survives "this is an ad". | Unbelievable magnitudes, unsupported superlatives. |
| 7 | **Objection handling** | 5 | Answers the top 1–2 reasons not to buy (price, trust, "is it for me"). | Ignores them. |
| 8 | **Offer & CTA** | 10 | Clear offer, risk reversal, a CTA that matches the read; headline + description pull their weight. | No offer, or a CTA that contradicts the story. |
| 9 | **Compliance & trust** | 5 | No disease/health claims it can't back, no fake reviews or invented experts, no fake urgency. | Cure claims, fake testimonials, countdown lies. |

**Bar:** an LMTY ad must score **≥ 85** and beat **≥ 95%** of the sampled library ads.
Readability (criterion 2) is measured with textstat, not eyeballed.
