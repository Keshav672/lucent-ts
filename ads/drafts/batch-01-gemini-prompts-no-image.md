# Batch #1 natives: Gemini image prompts (no product in the image)

Scene-only photos: no bottle, dropper or packaging. Paste each prompt into a NEW Gemini chat (image model).

## Ad 1 · The winter receipt · Main → save as ad1-cups-vs-mug.jpg

```
Create one photorealistic image, 4:5 portrait, 1080 x 1350.

SCENE: Top-down photo of a light oak kitchen table at dusk, lit by a ceiling light and the last blue light from a window. On the left: a messy pile of about twelve empty plain white paper to-go cups with plain white lids, a few tipped over, one with a used tea bag string hanging out, and two crumpled plain paper receipts with no readable text. On the right, clean and calm: one white ceramic mug of pale golden hot lemon tea with a thin lemon slice and a little real steam, on a folded linen napkin. Car keys and a phone face down near the cups. The contrast should feel obvious without any words.

PHOTO RULES: It must look like a real, candid photo taken on an iPhone 15 Pro by someone with a good eye, not an ad and not AI art. Natural light only (window, lamp or overhead light), true white balance, no orange filter, no golden glow. Real-world mess and real textures: wood grain, fabric texture, ceramic glaze, real steam, light film grain. Hands look relaxed with five fingers, real skin texture and short natural nails; faces cropped out or turned away. NO product in the photo: no bottles, droppers, jars, packaging or labels of any kind. No brand names, logos or readable text anywhere; cups, cars, receipts, uniforms and signs are plain and unbranded. No text overlays, captions, watermarks or borders.
```
