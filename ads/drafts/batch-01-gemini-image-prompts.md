# Batch #1 natives — Gemini image prompts

## How to use
1. Open Gemini and start a NEW chat. Use the image model (Nano Banana).
2. Attach your best real-looking bottle photo (lmty-drops-main.jpg) so every image uses the same bottle.
3. Paste the SETUP prompt once. Wait for Gemini to confirm.
4. Paste one ad prompt at a time, in the same chat. Each ad has a MAIN image and a BACKUP image.
5. Ask for 4:5 (feed). If Gemini gives a square, say: "Same image, 4:5 portrait, 1080 x 1350."
6. Regenerate until the label reads exactly right. Download each image and rename it to the file name shown.

Why no text on the images: a native ad has to look like a real photo someone took, not a designed ad. The copy does the selling. Gemini also misspells text in images.

## SETUP (paste once)

```
You are a lifestyle photographer shooting real, candid phone-style photos for a small brand's Facebook posts. Every image must look like a real photo taken on an iPhone 15 Pro by someone with a good eye. Nobody should be able to tell it is AI-generated.

THE PRODUCT (identical in every image, match the attached photo):
A 60 ml amber glass Boston-round dropper bottle with a black rubber-bulb dropper cap and a ribbed black collar. Matte cream paper label around the middle. Label text, spelled exactly:
"LMTY" (large bold sans-serif), a thin gold line, "WELLNESS DROPS" (small bold capitals), "Lemon flavor" (muted amber), a small round gold seal "ZERO SUGAR", bottom line "2 FL OZ · 60 mL · 30 SERVINGS".
No other words or logos on the bottle.

RULES FOR EVERY IMAGE:
- 4:5 portrait, 1080 x 1350.
- Natural light only (window, lamp, overhead office light). True white balance. No orange filter, no golden glow.
- Real-world mess: a crumb, a crooked napkin, a phone cable, a sticky note. Not styled perfectly.
- Real materials: glass reflections, refraction through the amber glass, paper grain, wood grain, fabric texture, light film grain.
- Hands: relaxed, five fingers, real skin texture, short natural nails. Faces cropped out or turned away unless asked.
- The bottle label is readable and faces the camera.
- NO brand names, logos or text anywhere except the LMTY label. Cups, cars, receipts, uniforms and signs are plain and unbranded.
- No text overlays, captions, watermarks or borders.

Reply "Ready" and wait for my next message.
```
